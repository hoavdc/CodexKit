# Email Introduction Templates

Use these templates when introducing yourself, connecting two people, or opening a business conversation.

## Formal External Introduction

**Subject:** Introduction: [Your Name] / [Company]

Dear [Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am [Role] at [Company]. I am reaching out because [specific reason].

I would appreciate the opportunity to [clear request], especially because [business context or relevance].

Would [date/time window] work for a short conversation?

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## Warm Referral Introduction

**Subject:** Introduction from [Mutual Contact]

Hi [Name],

[Mutual Contact] suggested I reach out. I am working on [brief context], and your experience with [specific area] seemed especially relevant.

I would value [specific ask: 15-minute call, feedback, introduction, review].

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Two-Person Introduction

**Subject:** Introducing [Person A] and [Person B]

Hi [Person A] and [Person B],

I wanted to introduce you both because [reason the connection is useful].

[Person A], [one-line context].
[Person B], [one-line context].

I will let you both take it from here.

Best,
[Your Name]
