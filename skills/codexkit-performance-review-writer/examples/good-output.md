# Good Output Example - Performance Review Writer

## Scenario

The user needs a mid-year review for a customer success manager who improved renewal process quality but needs stronger forecasting discipline.

## Good Output

```markdown
## Overall Summary
Maya is meeting expectations and showing strong progress toward senior-level account ownership. Her renewal process improvements created clearer customer handoffs and reduced last-minute escalations. The main development area for the next period is forecast discipline and earlier risk surfacing.

## Strengths

### Renewal Process Improvement
- **Situation:** The team had inconsistent renewal handoffs in Q1.
- **Behavior:** Maya created a renewal checklist, aligned support and account owners, and piloted it on 12 accounts.
- **Impact:** Escalations in the final two weeks before renewal decreased from 7 in Q1 to 3 in Q2.

## Development Area

### Forecast Discipline
- **Situation:** Two expansion opportunities were flagged late in the quarter.
- **Behavior:** Forecast notes were updated after customer confirmation rather than when risk signals first appeared.
- **Impact:** Leadership had less time to adjust the quarter plan.
- **Next Step:** Update opportunity risk notes weekly and flag red/yellow accounts in the Monday CS review.

## Goals For Next Period
1. Maintain weekly risk notes for all renewal accounts over $25K ARR by Friday each week.
2. Pilot an early-risk review with Sales by August 15.
3. Mentor one new CSM on the renewal checklist by quarter end.
```

## Why It Works

- Feedback is evidence-based.
- Strengths and gaps both use SBI structure.
- The development area is direct without being personal.
- Goals are measurable and connected to the role.
