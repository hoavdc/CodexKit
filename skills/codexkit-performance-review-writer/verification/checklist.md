# 4C Verification Checklist - Performance Review Writer

Use this checklist before sharing a performance review draft.

## Correctness - Is it right?

- [ ] Ratings, achievements, and development areas are supported by provided evidence.
- [ ] Feedback references observable behavior and outcomes, not personality.
- [ ] Prior goals are represented accurately.
- [ ] No compensation, promotion, legal, or disciplinary conclusion is invented.

## Completeness - Is it done?

- [ ] Overall summary, strengths, development areas, previous goals, new goals, and next steps are included.
- [ ] Strengths and gaps both use specific examples.
- [ ] New goals are SMART or have measurable success signals.
- [ ] Manager support actions are included where helpful.

## Context-fit - Does it match?

- [ ] Tone fits the review cycle and company culture.
- [ ] Expectations match the employee's role and level.
- [ ] Feedback is direct enough to be useful and respectful enough to be fair.
- [ ] The draft avoids over-indexing on the most recent events.

## Consequence - Is it safe to use?

- [ ] Could the wording create legal, HR, or fairness risk?
- [ ] Could the employee misunderstand expectations after reading this?
- [ ] Does any section need HR review before delivery?
- [ ] Are protected-class, health, family, or personal details excluded unless explicitly relevant and approved?

Final check: identify the most important expectation the employee should understand after reading.
