# Good Output Example - Email Composer

## Scenario

The user needs to follow up with a vendor who has not replied to a pricing request sent five business days ago.

## Prompt

```text
Draft a polite but firm follow-up email to Acme Corp asking for updated pricing by Friday. We sent the original request five business days ago and need the quote for a budget review next week.
```

## Good Output

```markdown
Subject: Follow-up on updated pricing request

Hi Jordan,

I wanted to follow up on our pricing request from last week. We are preparing for a budget review next week and need the updated quote by Friday to keep the review on schedule.

Could you send the revised pricing or let me know if you need anything else from our side?

Thanks,
Jane
```

## Why It Works

- The subject line states the purpose.
- The context explains why Friday matters.
- The ask is specific and easy to answer.
- The tone is professional without sounding aggressive.
- It does not invent a threat, commitment, or discount request.
