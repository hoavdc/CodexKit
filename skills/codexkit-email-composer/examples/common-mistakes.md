# Common Mistakes - Email Composer

## 1. Hiding the ask

**What goes wrong:** The email is polite but the reader cannot tell what action is needed.

**Fix:** Put the ask in one direct sentence and include timing when needed.

## 2. Over-escalating tone

**What goes wrong:** A routine follow-up sounds like a complaint or threat.

**Fix:** State the business consequence calmly before escalating.

## 3. Inventing commitments

**What goes wrong:** The draft says "as agreed" or "you promised" when the user did not provide that evidence.

**Fix:** Use source-backed wording such as "we discussed" only when the source supports it.

## 4. Using the wrong channel length

**What goes wrong:** A short Slack update becomes a long formal email, or an executive email is too casual.

**Fix:** Match length and tone to channel, recipient, and stakes.
