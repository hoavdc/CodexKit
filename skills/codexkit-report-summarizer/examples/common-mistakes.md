# Common Mistakes - Report Summarizer

## 1. Copying instead of summarizing

**What goes wrong:** The output repeats long source sentences and is almost as long as the report.

**Fix:** Group findings, preserve key numbers, and remove supporting detail unless needed for decisions.

## 2. Dropping caveats

**What goes wrong:** Limitations are omitted, making weak findings look certain.

**Fix:** Include a short caveats section for missing data, sample limits, or methodology concerns.

## 3. Mixing source facts with new advice

**What goes wrong:** The summary adds recommendations that the report did not make.

**Fix:** Label items as "source recommendation" or "inferred next step."

## 4. Ignoring audience

**What goes wrong:** An executive summary includes operational detail but no decision implication.

**Fix:** Lead with what changed, why it matters, and what decision is needed.
