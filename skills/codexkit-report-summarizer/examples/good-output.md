# Good Output Example - Report Summarizer

## Scenario

The user asks for an executive summary of a quarterly customer support operations report.

## Good Output

```markdown
## Executive Summary - Q2 Support Operations

### One-Line Takeaway
Support volume grew 18% quarter over quarter, but resolution speed improved because the team deflected more how-to requests through documentation.

### Key Findings
- Ticket volume increased from 8,400 to 9,912, mainly from onboarding-related questions.
- Median first response time improved from 4.2 hours to 2.9 hours after staffing changes.
- Billing tickets still have the slowest resolution time at 3.8 days median.
- CSAT fell from 91% to 87%, with pricing confusion appearing in 14% of negative comments.

### Implications
- Documentation is reducing simple support load, but billing experience remains a friction point.
- The CSAT decline is concentrated enough to assign a specific owner rather than treating it as a broad service problem.

### Recommended Actions From Source
1. Update billing FAQ and in-product invoice copy by July 15.
2. Route billing tickets to a specialized queue for a four-week pilot.
3. Review onboarding documentation search logs weekly.

### Caveats
The report does not segment CSAT by customer tier, so enterprise impact is unclear.
```

## Why It Works

- It keeps the original numbers visible.
- It separates findings, implications, actions, and caveats.
- It does not add recommendations beyond the source.
- It highlights the one thing leadership should verify next.
