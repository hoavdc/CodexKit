# 4C Verification Checklist - Report Summarizer

Use this checklist after producing a report summary.

## Correctness - Is it right?

- [ ] Key findings, numbers, dates, names, and recommendations match the source.
- [ ] No claim is added unless clearly labeled as inference.
- [ ] The source's confidence level, caveats, and limitations are preserved.
- [ ] Technical terms are not simplified into something inaccurate.

## Completeness - Is it done?

- [ ] The source purpose, thesis, findings, implications, and actions are covered.
- [ ] Major sections are represented or explicitly marked as omitted.
- [ ] Decisions requested from the reader are captured.
- [ ] Follow-up questions or missing information are listed.

## Context-fit - Does it match?

- [ ] Summary length matches the user's requested format.
- [ ] Detail level matches the reader's role.
- [ ] The summary leads with what the reader needs to know first.
- [ ] The output preserves enough context to prevent misleading shortcuts.

## Consequence - Is it safe to use?

- [ ] What could go wrong if the reader acts without opening the source?
- [ ] Are compliance, legal, financial, or safety-sensitive sections flagged for review?
- [ ] Are omitted sections low-risk enough to omit?
- [ ] Could the summary overstate certainty or hide uncertainty?

Final check: list one point the reader should verify in the original report.
