# Common Mistakes - Data Story Builder

## 1. Chart narration without insight

**What goes wrong:** The output says "conversion went up" but does not explain why it matters.

**Fix:** Add the business consequence and decision implication.

## 2. Causality overreach

**What goes wrong:** A correlation is presented as proof that a campaign or product change caused the result.

**Fix:** Use "likely," "consistent with," or "hypothesis" unless the design proves causality.

## 3. Missing caveats

**What goes wrong:** The story hides sample size, missing segments, or definition changes.

**Fix:** Include a caveat section and confidence label.

## 4. No action path

**What goes wrong:** The story ends with an observation.

**Fix:** End with a decision, experiment, owner action, or next analysis.
