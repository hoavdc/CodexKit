# Good Output Example - Data Story Builder

## Scenario

The user provides a dashboard showing that trial-to-paid conversion rose from 8.1% to 10.4% after onboarding changes.

## Good Output

```markdown
## Headline
Trial conversion improved after the onboarding change, but the evidence supports a strong hypothesis rather than a proven causal claim.

## What Changed
- Trial-to-paid conversion increased from 8.1% to 10.4% month over month.
- The largest improvement came from users who completed the setup checklist.
- Support tickets about setup decreased 22% during the same period.

## So What
The onboarding change appears to reduce early friction, which may be improving activation and conversion.

## Now What
1. Keep the checklist live for another month.
2. Segment the result by acquisition channel to rule out traffic-mix effects.
3. Add setup completion as a weekly product health metric.

## Caveat
This was not a randomized test, so seasonality and traffic mix could also explain part of the movement.
```

## Why It Works

- It avoids overstating causality.
- It connects metric movement to business consequence.
- It names the next analysis needed.
- It gives leaders a decision-ready narrative.
