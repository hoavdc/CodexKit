# 4C Verification Checklist - Data Story Builder

Use this checklist before sending a data narrative to stakeholders.

## Correctness - Is it right?

- [ ] All numbers, comparisons, and percentages match the source data.
- [ ] Causal language is justified by the evidence.
- [ ] Baselines, targets, time periods, and units are clear.
- [ ] Caveats and confidence levels are included where data is weak.

## Completeness - Is it done?

- [ ] The story answers: what changed, so what, now what.
- [ ] The narrative includes context, evidence, implication, and action.
- [ ] Material counter-signals or outliers are acknowledged.
- [ ] Recommended next steps are tied to owners or decisions where possible.

## Context-fit - Does it match?

- [ ] The level of detail matches the audience.
- [ ] The story is framed around the stakeholder's decision, not the analyst's curiosity.
- [ ] Technical terms are translated when the audience is non-technical.
- [ ] The tone is appropriately cautious when evidence is directional.

## Consequence - Is it safe to use?

- [ ] What bad decision could result from overstating certainty?
- [ ] Could a metric be optimized at the expense of a guardrail?
- [ ] Could seasonality, data quality, or definition changes explain the movement?
- [ ] Should this be positioned as a finding, hypothesis, or recommendation?

Final check: label the main claim as fact, interpretation, or hypothesis.
