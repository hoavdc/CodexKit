# Team Onboarding Workspace

Use this workspace to coordinate onboarding for a new employee, contractor, or team member without starting from a blank document.

## Best For

- HR partners
- hiring managers
- team leads
- mentors or buddies
- operations teams supporting new hires

## Included Files

- `01-getting-started.md` - onboarding context, owners, and first setup decisions
- `02-checklist.md` - pre-start, day-one, week-one, and first-30-days checklist

- `codexkit-onboarding-checklist-creator`
- `codexkit-onboarding-plan-creator`
- `codexkit-meeting-summary-generator`
