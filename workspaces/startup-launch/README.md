# Startup Launch Workspace

Use this workspace to turn an early business idea into a practical validation and launch plan.

## Best For

- non-technical founders
- small business owners
- solopreneurs
- product teams testing a new market

## Included Files

- `01-validate-idea.md` - problem, audience, assumptions, competitors, validation plan
- `02-business-plan-outline.md` - business model, GTM, milestones, and operating risks
